1. Overview and Narrative Expansion
This whitepaper architecture was not conceived from the outset as a massive single integrated safety system, but rather originated from a clear, single task: Edge Safety Device Architecture for Press Brakes, Shears, and Forming Machinery (Press-Brake-Shear Whitepaper).
In analyzing real-world hazards at processing sites—such as pinching, scattering, and entanglement—the defensive techniques established for a single hazard model naturally expanded and diverged into a wider variety of machine tool environments, including rotating machinery proximity sensing, NC-controlled clamp interference, and recoil mitigation for portable cutting tools.
 * Initial Starting Point: Establishment of a refined edge safety architecture centered on press brakes, shearing machines, and power presses (Press-Brake-Shear Whitepaper)
 * Universal Branching: Separation into a dedicated universal project (Universal Series No. 1) directly under the master hub, considering the specialized nature of rotating shaft entanglement and proximity sensing mechanisms
 * Domain Expansion: Specialization by hazard model into stationary traditional machine tools (lathes, mills, etc.), NC sheet metal control (turret punch), and portable grinding/cutting tools (grinders)

3. Sub-Whitepaper Summaries by Hazard Expansion Narrative
 * Press Brake, Shear, and Power Press Safety Whitepaper (Press-Brake-Shear)
   * Key Content: An architecture that defends horizontal and vertical linear motion pinching and cutting hazard zones via vision and edge sensors; serves as the patent-verified starting baseline.
   * Reference: Officially declared as Sub-Whitepaper No. 1 under the Stationary Machine Tools Division.
 * Rotating Machinery Body Proximity and Entanglement Detection Whitepaper (Universal-Rotating)
   * Key Content: A mechanism detecting human body/clothing entanglement risks near high-speed rotating shafts and spindles via ultra-low-latency edge computing.
   * Reference: Branching off into a universal common module not restricted to specific machine tools, operating as an independent project directly under the master hub.
 * Lathe, Milling Machine, Drill Press, and Grinding Machine Whitepaper (Lathe-Milling-Drillpress)
   * Key Content: Sub-Whitepaper No. 2 under the Stationary Machine Tools Division; a multi-sensor fusion safety architecture designed to mitigate chip scattering, tool contact, and workpiece ejection risks during rotational machining.
 * NC Turret Punch Clamp Interference Avoidance Whitepaper (Sheet-Metal-NC-Punch)
   * Key Content: A system that dynamically calculates and avoids collisions between high-speed moving NC clamps and punching dies to prevent operator zone intrusion and structural impact.
 * Grinder and Portable Cutting Tool Safety Whitepaper (Grinder-Portable-Cutting-Tool)
   * Key Content: A portable edge architecture designed to rapidly mitigate injury from grinding wheel breakage, debris scattering, kickback, and recoil in handheld and small-scale machinery.
